Privacy policy

Hermes is a personal application used only by its owner. It accesses the owner's own Google account data (such as Gmail, Calendar, Drive, Docs, Sheets, Contacts and Tasks) solely to carry out tasks the owner requests.

  • No data from other users is collected; the app has no other users.
  • Google user data is not sold, shared with third parties for advertising, or used for any purpose other than the owner's own tasks.
  • Data is processed on the owner's own computer and by the AI model provider the owner has chosen to carry out requested tasks.
  • Access can be revoked at any time at myaccount.google.com/permissions.
